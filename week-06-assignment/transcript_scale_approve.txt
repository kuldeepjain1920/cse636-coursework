## 📋 Postmortem Draft — INC-002: order-svc CPU Saturation

- **🚨 Incident trigger:** `order-svc` sustained CPU utilization above 85% for 15+ minutes (peak 91.56%), causing error rate to climb to 14.9% and p99 latency to degrade to ~3.7 seconds — directly impacting order processing SLOs.

- **🔎 Root cause confirmed:** CPU saturation (per upstream RCA pipeline, not a deployment regression). Corroborated by logs showing thread pool at 98% utilization and request queue depth exceeding 500, indicating the existing 4 replicas were unable to absorb incoming load.

- **⚡ Remediation taken:** After a clean `dry_run_scale` validation, `order-svc` was horizontally scaled out from **4 → 7 replicas** (within the max of 10), targeting ~60% CPU utilization to restore headroom. No rollback or code change was required.

- **💰 Error budget impact:** Error budget remaining was at a low **0.34** at time of remediation — the SRE team should review SLO burn rate and consider whether a freeze or capacity pre-provisioning policy is warranted to prevent budget exhaustion.

- **🔁 Follow-up actions recommended:** (1) Investigate whether a load spike, traffic pattern change, or upstream dependency drove the saturation; (2) evaluate HPA (Horizontal Pod Autoscaler) tuning so future ramp-ups trigger scale-out *before* CPU exceeds 85%; (3) review JVM/GC settings given the elevated 340ms GC pause that may compound latency under load.
